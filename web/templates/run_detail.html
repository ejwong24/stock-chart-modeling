Honesty check: Deflated Sharpe Ratio

Why this matters: The original document claimed "100th percentile vs 100 random seeds" — but it tried 72 model configurations to find that winner. Under the null hypothesis, P(at least one of 72 configs beats p95) ≈ 97.5%. The Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014) corrects the Sharpe threshold to account for this multiple-testing inflation. Significance > 0.95 means the result survives the correction.
