What is a "label"? The pipeline trains a classifier to predict whether a stock's price will rise by at least threshold% in the next horizon trading days from any given anchor date. The original document's headline was horizon=40, threshold=25%.

What labels combinations were tested in the original document?

The original tested 36 labels: H ∈ {5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40} × T ∈ {5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%}. Plus 2 ranking variants per label (score_only and risk-adjusted) = 72 configurations total. Trying many configs and reporting the best one is exactly what the deflated Sharpe ratio penalizes. If you want a single honest result, pick one (horizon, threshold) before looking at any data and stick with it.

How to interpret different (horizon, threshold) pairs
  • Short H, low T (5d, 5%): label fires often (~30–40% positive rate). Easier to predict, but small per-trade alpha is eaten by costs.
  • Long H, high T (40d, 25%): label fires rarely (~5–10% positive rate). Harder to predict, but successful predictions are very profitable.
  • Original best: 40d / 25%, "score only" — but see Flaws & Fixes for why this headline number is misleading.
